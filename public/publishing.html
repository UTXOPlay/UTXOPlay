Publish your game on UTXOPlay

Your game, your Myelin instance, your players. Start free: there is no listing fee, no deposit and no payment transaction for free launches.

Open Studio →

1. Make a private draft

Connect your CKB-compatible wallet and choose Publish a game. Add a title, description, controls, version and HTTPS game URL. Saving a draft does not change your live release.

Developer and publisher credits

The developer is the original creator or studio. The publisher manages this Store listing. Enter their names separately, including original authors for ports. Both names are required before publishing; private drafts may be incomplete. Add an optional original-project HTTPS link and review the credits in the final publishing step. Leave the developer CKB wallet blank if it is unknown; do not substitute your own address. Your publisher wallet is taken from the signed-in project owner and appears publicly with the listing. Credit names and developer wallets are publisher-supplied attribution, not verified ownership claims. The payout address is configured separately and does not change publishing permissions. Save edits as a draft and republish to update public credits. Publisher collections group listings by the publishing wallet. Developer collections keep publisher-supplied claims within that publisher’s catalog. Purchase receipts preserve the credits recorded when the order was created.

2. Connect your hosted instance

Run your own Myelin game runtime behind the UTXOPlay vendor adapter. Supply the public HTTPS adapter origin on port 443; host your game on that same origin. Keep the runtime itself private.

Generate a launch key in Studio and copy the server environment snippet. Install Node 22.23+, the Store repository dependencies and your game runtime. Configure the adapter with the project ID, verification token, server-only launch key, game URL, private runtime URL/token, session secret and web root. Run:

node --env-file=/private/path/vendor.env vendor/adapter.mjs

Proxy your HTTPS domain to the adapter’s private port 8800, including WebSocket upgrades. The complete hosting guide includes the environment template, Caddy example, runtime API contract and launch protocol. Myelin integration is game-specific; supplying a URL alone does not port a game.

Choose Save & check connection. The Store verifies public configuration, project/key matching and reported runtime readiness. This is an interoperability check, not certification of the game’s code or content.

3. Start free, or set a one-time price

Free to play is selected by default. Publish free after the connection check. You pay your hosting costs; the Store charges nothing for free listings or launches. Guests can play. Wallet players retain their claimed free access if you later add a price.

For paid access, enter a CKB mainnet receive address, price, support URL and refund-policy URL. You receive 80%; the Store receives 20%. Both outputs are paid in a single mainnet transaction. The buyer separately pays the network fee. Check settlement to see the minimum price required by the actual receive addresses; two standard 61 CKB cells need a total of at least 305 CKB. Other address types can require more.

Paid publication requires the Store’s receive address to be configured. If it is not ready, save your paid draft or publish free. No funds are requested just to create a project.

What players buy

One purchase gives that wallet account access with no scheduled expiry. This is not a transferable NFT or a recurring subscription. The vendor must continue hosting the game; the Store cannot guarantee permanent third-party uptime. Show clear availability, support and refund terms.

The player reviews the quote and estimated network fee, then explicitly signs in their wallet. Access unlocks after 24 CKB confirmations. Purchases saves the receipt and checks confirmation in the background. Retrying reuses the reserved transaction; never send a second manual payment to resolve a pending receipt.

Manage releases and revenue

Save edits as a draft, then explicitly republish. Existing receipts keep their original price and addresses. Unlisting stops new discovery and purchases while preserving existing owners’ access; keep their instance running. Sales & receipts shows confirmed vendor revenue, paid directly to your wallet. There is no Store withdrawal queue.

Refunds are handled with the vendor under their policy. Automatic refunds, escrow and disputes are not implemented. Fiber remains an optional testnet extension separate from these CKB purchases.
